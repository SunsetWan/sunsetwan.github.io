Xiaozhua Stickers 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日

适用范围

本政策适用于 Xiaozhua Stickers App 及其 iMessage 贴纸扩展。

应用数据

贴纸随应用提供。应用不要求注册或登录，不读取照片库、通讯录或位置信息，也不收集、存储或向开发者传输个人信息。

应用不包含广告、第三方分析或追踪 SDK，不提供照片上传或用户内容发布功能。

信息服务

贴纸的发送、送达以及聊天内容处理由系统信息服务负责，适用 Apple 的服务条款和隐私政策。本应用的开发者不会接收聊天内容。

邮件反馈

如果主动通过电子邮件联系开发者，开发者会收到邮件地址及邮件中提供的内容，并将其用于回复和处理反馈。请不要发送密码、验证码或私人聊天内容。

联系

bobs_note_app@outlook.com

Privacy Policy

Updated November 7, 2026

This policy applies to Xiaozhua Stickers and its iMessage sticker extension.

Stickers are included with the app. The app does not require an account, access your photo library, contacts, or location, or collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer. It contains no ads, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, photo uploads, or user content publishing features.

Sending stickers, message delivery, and processing conversation content are handled by the system messaging service under Apple's applicable terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive conversation content.

If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to provide, and uses it to respond to and handle your inquiry. Please do not include passwords, verification codes, or private conversation content.

Contact: bobs_note_app@outlook.com.
